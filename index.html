About Us

Our journey, vision and commitment

Welcome to Thodukayil Group. We are a diversified business group with operations across multiple industries. Our business interests include petroleum distribution, construction, international exports and plantation management.


Our commitment is to build lasting relationships, deliver quality services and create sustainable growth through integrity, innovation and excellence.
